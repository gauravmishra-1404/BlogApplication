Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

The short version. Bodh Sea stores the account details you give us and the content you create. We don't sell your data, we don't run advertising trackers, and we don't share your information with third parties except the handful of infrastructure providers listed below that are needed to actually run the service. Your drafts are private until you publish them.

What we collect

Account details
Your name, email address, and a password — which is stored only as a one-way hash, never as the password itself. Optionally an avatar and cover image if you upload them.
Content you create
Posts, Shorts, comments, replies, tags, and any images or video you attach. Drafts and scheduled items are included here — they're stored the same way, they're just not visible to anyone else until published.
Activity within the service
Reactions, bookmarks, reposts, follows, and view counts on posts and Shorts. These exist to make the product work (a bookmark list needs to know what you bookmarked) and to show authors how their work is doing.
Technical data
A session cookie so you stay logged in between pages, and standard server logs. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising cookies.

Why we collect it

  • To run your account — authenticating you, keeping you logged in, and verifying your email address so accounts can't be created in your name.
  • To provide the service — storing and displaying what you publish, and delivering it to people who follow you.
  • To notify you — telling you when someone follows, comments on, or reacts to your work, through whichever channels you've enabled.
  • To keep it working and safe — diagnosing errors and investigating abuse.

Where it's stored

Bodh Sea runs on Amazon Web Services in the Asia Pacific (Mumbai) region. Specifically:

  • Account and content records are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Amazon RDS), which is not publicly reachable from the internet.
  • Images and video you upload are stored in Amazon S3. Media attached to published content is served publicly — anyone with the URL can view it, which is what makes it displayable in a feed.
  • Login sessions are held in Amazon ElastiCache (Redis).
  • All traffic to the site is encrypted in transit over HTTPS.

Third parties we share data with

Only where it's required to deliver a feature you've used:

  • Amazon Web Services — hosting, storage, and database infrastructure.
  • SendGrid (Twilio) — sending transactional email such as verification links, password resets, and notification emails. They receive your email address and the message content.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivering push notifications, if you enable them.

We do not sell personal data, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices

  • Notifications — choose which channels (in-app, email, push) you want, from your account settings.
  • Your content — edit or delete any post, Short, comment, draft, or bookmark at any time. Deleting content removes it from the service.
  • Export — download any published post as a PDF, at any time, without asking us.
  • Deletion — to delete your account and associated data, contact us at the address on the contact page.

Retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account exists. Content you delete is removed from the service. Server logs and backups are retained for a limited period for operational and recovery purposes and then age out.

Children

Bodh Sea isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where the change affects how your data is used, notify you in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or want your data deleted? See the contact page.
