Contact

We read everything that comes in

Bodh Sea is a small project, so there's no ticket queue and no phone tree — email reaches a person directly, and we'll get back to you.

Where to write

bodhsea@gmail.com

One inbox for everything — support, privacy requests, security reports and abuse. To help it get handled faster, start your subject line with the kind of message it is:

Support
Something broken, something confusing, or an idea for what should exist next.
Privacy
To request a copy of your data, or to have your account and its content deleted. Please write from the email address on the account so we can confirm it's you.
Security
Vulnerability reports — see the security page for what to include and what we ask of you while testing.
Abuse
Content that breaks the terms of service. Include a link to the post or Short so we can find it quickly.

Before you write

  • Forgotten password? Use the reset link on the login page — it's faster than emailing us.
  • Didn't get your verification email? Check spam first, then request a new link from the login page.
  • Want your writing out? You don't need us — any published post exports as a PDF from the post itself.

Response times

This is a small operation, not a 24/7 support desk. We aim to reply within a few days, and faster for security reports and account-access problems.
