Two currents, one sea

Write the long way. Or the fast way.

Posts and Shorts aren't bolted together — each has its own feed, its own rhythm, and its own reason to open the app.

Posts

For ideas that need room

  • Rich text, tags, and up to 4 images or a video
  • Save a draft, or schedule it for later — real scheduling, not a reminder
  • Export any published post as a clean PDF
Shorts

For the ones worth a tap

  • A full-screen, swipe-through feed — one Short after the next
  • Tap to pause, a watch-progress bar, autoplay that respects your finger
  • Every Short has its own real link — share the exact one, not the whole feed
